# timburley.com — Editorial & Site Rules

1. **Always write site copy in the first person (`I`, `me`, `my`).**
   - Never refer to Tim in the third person in site copy, buttons, hints, microcopy, or footers (e.g. never "Send it straight to Tim", "You work directly with Tim", "More about Tim", or "led by Tim Burley").
   - Use "Tim Burley" only for the brand wordmark/masthead, page `<title>`/`©` line, photo captions/alt text, and inside quoted third-party testimonials where a client is naturally speaking about Tim.
2. **Branding & Positioning:** Brand the site as **Tim Burley** (not `timburley.com`), with the supporting line **Creating great names**. Contact email is `names@timburley.com`. Lead the narrative with **20 years of hands-on name development experience** (naming craft, linguistic judgment, trademark fluency); position agency leadership as a supporting benefit focused on leading teams and delivering positive outcomes.
3. **Language:** Use crisp British English (`organisation`, `colour`, `behaviour`, `recognise`, `favour`) and plain, direct, zero-fluff editorial prose.
4. **Visual Palette, Typography & Photography:** Warm architectural paper (`#F6F5F0`), deep ink (`#111110`), warm stone (`#ECEAE2`), with an **ultra-restrained Editorial Vermilion accent (`#C93B2B` on light, `#EB5E4D` on dark)** (~3% canvas: block CTAs stay ink at rest and animate a left-to-right vermilion fill on hover; text links animate a left-to-right vermilion underline on hover; vermilion marks active nav underlines, eyebrow dots, editorial numbers, pitfall quote bars, brief progress bar, live trading dots, and upright display emphasis). Display typography uses **Instrument Sans** (Medium `500`, upright — no italicised headline phrases) paired with **Inter** for body copy and **IBM Plex Mono** for metadata. Keep portraits in **full colour** (no grayscale filters) and strictly **one image** on `/about` (the coastal beach portrait).
5. **Pitfalls:** Reflect the six real pitfalls from *"Your Name Here"* faithfully on `/process#pitfalls` without linking out to `believein.net`.
6. **Work:** Every naming project gets its own dedicated page under `/work/<slug>/` (never a slide-over panel).
7. **Privacy:** Only files inside `public/` are deployed; never commit or publish private notes/transcripts from the repository root.
