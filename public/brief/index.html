Guided Naming Brief · ~5 Minutes

Let’s get the essentials on the table.

Six short prompts designed to sharpen your thinking around what matters—context, decision-making and trademark reality—so I can send back a clear, simple proposal.

Step 01 of 06
01 · Commissioning Route

How are we working together?

Choose the route that best fits, and leave your details so I know who I’m replying to.

02 · Scope

What are we naming?

Pick the closest fit—if it spans more than one, note it in the sector field below.

03 · Context & Ambition

Tell the story in plain English.

No polished copy needed—just what the offer does, why now, and what a great name needs to unlock.

04 · The Approval Pipeline

Who approves, agrees and decides?

A clear decision structure is half the battle. Let me know who is in the room so we design the process to build real confidence.

05 · Trademark & Timing

Legal partner, markets & timeline.

Understanding trademark geography and target dates helps shape the scope cleanly from day one.

06 · Proposal-Ready Summary

Your naming brief is ready.

Review everything below. Send it straight to me by email, copy it to share internally, or print/save as a PDF.
