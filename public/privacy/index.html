Privacy Notice · Updated November 2026

Plain-English privacy, no cookies required.

This website is run by me, Tim Burley, an independent brand naming consultant based in the UK. It is built to be light, straightforward and respectful of your privacy.

Zero cookies and no tracking

This website sets zero cookies and uses zero browser storage (localStorage or sessionStorage). It runs no advertising pixels and does not track you across other sites, which is why there is no cookie pop-up or consent banner blocking the page.

Cookieless analytics

I use Vercel Web Analytics to understand broad usage patterns, such as overall visitor numbers, which case studies are read most often and where referrals come from. It uses no cookies, stores nothing on your device and discards anonymised request hashes automatically so individual visitors cannot be identified or followed across days.

Self-hosted and first-party

Typography (Instrument Sans and IBM Plex Mono) is self-hosted directly on this site alongside the static Website Carbon link in the footer, so browsing here makes no calls to external font CDNs or third-party tracking scripts.

Naming briefs and email enquiries

If you complete the brief form on Start a Brief or email names@timburley.com, I receive the details you choose to share: typically your name, organisation, email address and project context. Brief submissions are delivered via Resend straight to my inbox.

I use those details solely to reply to your enquiry, prepare a proposal or carry out agreed naming work. I never sell, rent or pass your information on for third-party marketing, and I will never add you to an email mailing list.

How long I keep information and your rights

I keep enquiry and project correspondence only for as long as it is useful for our conversation, active client work or basic UK business records.

Under UK GDPR, you can ask to view, correct or delete any personal information I hold about you at any time by emailing names@timburley.com. You also have the right to raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk ↗.
