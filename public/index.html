01 · Point of View

Coming up with a name is only half of the job.

AI can dump a thousand plausible-sounding words into a bucket and fish a few out. It has no idea which ones will survive a trademark examiner, align a leadership team, or hold meaning ten years from now.

HALF ONE · THE NAME

Judgment over volume.

Effective naming is not a word-generation game. It requires strategic discipline, linguistic sensitivity and an early understanding of how trademark registration actually works across classes and territories.

Whether the answer is an invented mark, a rooted place name, or an unmistakable plain-English phrase, the craft is knowing what will endure for the lifetime of the business.

HALF TWO · THE DECISION

Confidence without compromise.

Without a structured process, internal naming discussions quickly collapse into “who likes what best?”—ending either in stalemate or, worse, the safe middle ground: the least-worst option everyone can grudgingly accept.

Twenty-five years leading client services taught me that steering the room—evaluating, aligning, deciding and clearing—is where a naming practice truly pays for itself.

A Case in Point

Sometimes the right name is staring you in the face.

When we named South West Food & Drink—the body promoting food and drink across the south west of Great Britain—it didn’t require tortured cleverness. It was clear, honest and right. What elevated it was the supporting work: connecting the name to memory, taste and sense of place through the “Sensational” launch campaign. A strong name opens the door; the process and narrative around it make it unforgettable.

04 · The Model

Three ingredients for a name that actually gets chosen.

Naming projects don’t need bloated teams. They need clarity on who is creating, who is deciding, and who is clearing the trademark.

01

One senior namer

You work directly with Tim across briefing, territory exploration, name creation and presentation—keeping the process tight, candid and efficient.

02

An agreed approval pipeline

Before we start, we agree who inputs, who aligns and who makes the final call—eliminating late-stage vetoes and circular taste debates.

03

Your trademark legal partner

Your IP firm handles formal registration; I pre-screen and shape the shortlist alongside them so clearance moves cleanly and cost-effectively.
