About me

Twenty years leading name development. Built for names that endure.

A writer and specialist namer with two decades of hands-on name development behind me—working directly with founders, organisations and partner agencies to create great names and guide teams to positive outcomes.

Great naming is a specialist craft—not a brainstorm, and never a side-project.

For the past twenty years, I have led name development projects across twenty-five years in agency land—running naming, brand strategy, copywriting and client-services functions across brand, design, digital and marketing agencies including Believe in, Organic and Design Stable.

Over those two decades I have honed the naming chops that separate lasting names from forgettable ones: sharp linguistic judgment, disciplined territory strategy, and a practical command of how trademark registration actually works—across healthcare mergers, district heating networks, SaaS platforms, superyachts, challenger food brands, public-sector partnerships, housing developments and commercial typefaces for FontSmith (later acquired by Monotype).

My agency leadership background—leading multi-disciplinary teams and delivering positive outcomes—is an added advantage in the room. It means I know how to keep senior stakeholders aligned, cut through opinion-based deadlocks, and steer a shortlist cleanly to a confident decision. Working directly with me strips away agency layers: you get twenty years of hands-on naming experience, first-hand, from brief to final sign-off.

In 2026, alongside launching my standalone naming practice, I also co-founded Committed Citizens ↗ with my business partner Ben Scoggins—my former colleague and Chief Executive at the digital agency Organic, where we worked together for six years.

Clear Boundaries

Four things this practice doesn’t do.

Clarity about how I work—and where my role ends—keeps projects honest, fast and cost-effective.

  • No AI name-slop buckets I don’t feed prompts into an LLM and hand you 400 derivative words hoping one sticks. Naming takes human taste, commercial context and judgment.
  • No junior hand-offs There is no account-layer telephone game. You brief, challenge and decide directly with me from start to finish.
  • No amateur legal advice Formal trademark registration belongs with a qualified trademark attorney. My role is to understand the register deeply enough to give your IP partner clean, registrable candidates.
  • No bloated 90-slide theatre Just clear strategic criteria, disciplined territories, rigorously edited shortlists and straight-talking guidance through the decision.
