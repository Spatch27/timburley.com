How Meddo came to be.

01 · The Brief

The challenge

Created while I was at the brand consultancy Design Stable, the client was bringing together local property maintenance specialists united by a commitment to genuinely positive customer service, in a sector dominated by stiff, overly buttoned-down trade brands.

02 · The Story

Inside the naming process

We wanted customers to feel they could breathe easy and know their home was in safe hands. That led to the calm, restorative image of a “meadow”; the alternative spelling created a modern, ownable, warm and distinctive brand that sat apart from traditional maintenance firms.

03 · Process & Decision

Steering alignment & clearance

The distinctive spelling unlocked clean brand ownership and planted a light, human seed that carried effortlessly across the wider visual identity and launch rollout.

04 · The Outcome

Why the name works

Meddo established a lighter, friendlier and far more engaging presence in a traditionally functional market.
