How Yomira came to be.

01 · The Brief

The challenge

Working again with Robin Jaffray and Tim Doust at R&D, we partnered with Yomira’s two founders, Neil and Amanda, to create a brand name capable of entering an exceptionally demanding international luxury market, curating tailored superyacht charter experiences for discerning travellers exploring the world’s oceans.

02 · The Story

Inside the naming process

Yomira was coined by bringing together “Yo” as a contraction of “your” and “Mira” derived from the Sanskrit for ocean, literally evoking “your ocean”. It captures the promise of a voyage designed entirely around the individual client, without ever lapsing into nautical clichés.

03 · Process & Decision

Steering alignment & clearance

In a crowded global luxury market, securing both legal distinctiveness and clean digital ownership was vital. Creating an ownable coined mark enabled us to secure yomira.com and build a brand foundation with real international poise.

04 · The Outcome

Why the name works

Yomira gave the new venture a distinctive, creative and effortlessly international name, equally at home on a superyacht masthead, digital flagship and global luxury press.
