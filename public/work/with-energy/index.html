How With Energy came to be.

01 · The Brief

The challenge

Building directly on the relationship from Loka, the client wanted a standardised energy brand that could drop into many different communities across the country, eliminating the need to invent and register a separate brand for every local scheme.

02 · The Story

Inside the naming process

“With Energy” turned a simple, disarming preposition into a flexible brand platform. It worked both as a clear company name and as a collaborative statement about working with local communities, bringing warmth, momentum and enjoyment to how people engage with their local energy provider.

03 · Process & Decision

Steering alignment & clearance

Engineered as a repeatable naming solution able to flex cleanly across diverse developments and resident audiences without losing personal warmth.

04 · The Outcome

Why the name works

With Energy created an approachable, scalable brand architecture built to travel cleanly from community to community.
