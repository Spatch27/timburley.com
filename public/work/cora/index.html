How Cora came to be.

01 · The Brief

The challenge

Robin Jaffray and Tim Doust, the senior agency leaders behind the brand consultancy R&D Partners, brought me in to lead name development for the merger of Healthshare and Connect Health. Working alongside R&D, creative specialists and the leadership teams from both legacy organisations, we needed a single unifying name that honoured what patients already valued while giving the newly combined business a warm, credible platform for growth.

02 · The Story

Inside the naming process

Reviewing patient feedback across both businesses revealed one consistent theme: people deeply valued the personal level of treatment they received and the genuine commitment to patient wellbeing. A short, memorable female name gave us an immediate human presence, while “Cora” also carries a natural etymological root back to the human heart. Together, those qualities created a warm, personal, unmistakably caring name for the merged organisation.

03 · Process & Decision

Steering alignment & clearance

I developed and presented the shortlist across both leadership teams, guided stakeholder alignment past legacy-brand attachment, and worked directly alongside the company’s legal partner to secure formal trademark registration prior to launch.

04 · The Outcome

Why the name works

Cora launched cleanly with trademark protection in place, giving the combined organisation a compelling, human brand name that stands apart from clinical healthcare conventions.
