How FS Silas came to be.

01 · The Brief

The challenge

Fontsmith was one of the world’s leading independent type foundries prior to its acquisition by Monotype. At Believe in we launched several typefaces for the firm, including FS Silas, which included the naming, launch identity, type specimen, print support and promotional imagery.

02 · The Story

Inside the naming process

We built the name and launch campaign around a crisp theme of espionage and intrigue, framing the typeface launch like a classified secret dossier supported by teaser print materials and specimen artefacts. “Silas” carried the exact air of quiet intelligence, literary poise and sharpest graphic character needed for the letterforms themselves.

03 · Process & Decision

Steering alignment & clearance

Typeface names live permanently inside designers’ font menus worldwide, so the name had to sit naturally within Fontsmith’s “FS” foundry architecture.

04 · The Outcome

Why the name works

FS Silas launched with memorable creative intrigue and remains part of the celebrated Fontsmith library under Monotype.
