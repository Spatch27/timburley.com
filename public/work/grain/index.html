How Grain came to be.

01 · The Brief

The challenge

Created at Believe in for a joint venture between a property group and a telecoms provider, Grain was built to challenge legacy telecoms giants, partnering with housebuilders to install end-to-end fibre-optic networks directly into new residential developments.

02 · The Story

Inside the naming process

Because the network installed fibre from end-to-end, we drew inspiration from “To see a World in a Grain of Sand”, the opening line of William Blake’s poem, “Auguries of Innocence.” Silicon becomes optical fibre, hair-thin strands of glass capable of carrying vast bandwidth into modern homes. “Grain” gave the business a poetic, genuine origin story right as full-fibre was taking off, without tying the brand to jargon that would quickly date.

03 · Process & Decision

Steering alignment & clearance

Pairing an evocative, human lead word (“Grain”) with a clear functional anchor (“Connect”) gave housebuilders and residents immediate clarity while standing miles apart from hyperbolic telecoms clichés.

04 · The Outcome

Why the name works

Grain continues to connect thousands of new-build homes across the UK to ultrafast broadband.
