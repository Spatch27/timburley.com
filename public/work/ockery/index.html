How Ockery came to be.

01 · The Brief

The challenge

Seafood producer Ocean Crown (owned by Scandia Foods) needed a new consumer brand for a range of fishcakes made from fresh, honest ingredients and simple recipes.

02 · The Story

Inside the naming process

“Ockery” emerged as a rich, tactile phonetic word with historical connections back to a traditional stone dwelling, instantly evoking roots, coastal simplicity and back-to-basics provenance.

03 · Process & Decision

Steering alignment & clearance

Crucially, its distinctiveness meant it cleared cleanly and allowed us to secure ockery.com, giving the product range an ownable, memorable home on shelf and online.

04 · The Outcome

Why the name works

A uniquely British, grounded food brand name brimming with warmth, texture and natural culinary honesty.
