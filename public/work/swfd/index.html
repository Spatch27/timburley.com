A unifying regional brand name championing food and drink across the South West of England, proving that sometimes the obvious solution is the right one.

How South West Food & Drink came to be.

01 · The Brief

The challenge

Food promotion group Taste of the West commissioned the agency Red Rocket to create a new unifying brand to champion food and drink from across the South West of England, backed by funding from the Regional Development Agency (RDA).

02 · The Story

Inside the naming process

Sometimes the obvious solution is the right one. Rather than forcing cleverness onto a regional body that needed immediate clarity and authority, “South West Food & Drink” united producers and buyers under a name that was clear, honest and right. To accompany the main brand, we created a launch campaign built around the word “Sensational”, grounded in research showing that people strongly associated the region with tourism and memorable experiences, connecting South West food and drink to the sensations people enjoyed and held on to when visiting.

03 · Process & Decision

Steering alignment & clearance

Effective naming requires the judgement to know when plain-English clarity beats invention, and how to strengthen a straightforward name with supporting narrative and campaign thinking that gives it genuine emotional pull.

04 · The Outcome

Why the name works

South West Food & Drink established an unmistakable unifying flagship for the region’s producers, brought vividly to life through a “Sensational” launch rooted in memory, taste and sense of place.
