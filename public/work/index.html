Beyond Cleverness · Regional Identity

South West Food & Drink, and the power of “Sensational”.

Not every naming brief calls for an ingenious solution. When creating the name for the body promoting food and drink across the south west of Great Britain, South West Food & Drink was right there in plain sight: clear, authoritative and immediately understood by producers and buyers alike.

Its real power came from the launch campaign built around the word “Sensational”, drawing on research showing people associated the South West with tourism and experiences, and connecting the region’s produce to the sensations people enjoyed and held on to long after visiting.
