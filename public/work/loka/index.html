How Loka came to be.

01 · The Brief

The challenge

Created for urban regenerator Knight Dragon, Loka serves the major residential and commercial redevelopment surrounding The O2 London on Greenwich Peninsula, operating from a central energy centre delivering heat across the entire community.

02 · The Story

Inside the naming process

District heating is often presented in dry, technical utility language. “Loka” brought together the two defining truths of the network in one short, approachable word: “local” and “low carbon”, communicating both dedicated neighbourhood service and meaningful carbon reduction.

03 · Process & Decision

Steering alignment & clearance

We shaped a name that could live effortlessly alongside ambitious urban architecture while feeling transparent, modern and trustworthy to residents receiving their energy bills every month.

04 · The Outcome

Why the name works

Loka continues to serve customers across Greenwich Peninsula today, proving that low-carbon infrastructure can carry warmth and clarity.
