How Hemiko came to be.

01 · The Brief

The challenge

When a private equity firm acquired Pinnacle Power, the business needed to step out from the wider Pinnacle Group and chart an ambitious independent course. Working directly with the client leadership, we set out to create an innovative, distinctive name capable of establishing a major British force in district heating.

02 · The Story

Inside the naming process

“Hemiko” grew from the concept of Heat Energy Markets (HEM), capturing a forward-looking vision of how communities can both generate and consume low-carbon heat locally. While district heating has long been established across continental Europe, Hemiko needed a fresh, modern name built to champion its growth across the UK.

03 · Process & Decision

Steering alignment & clearance

Following PE investment, the new company required rapid alignment across investors and management alongside clean trademark clearance, giving the spun-out business a scalable name free of legacy utility baggage.

04 · The Outcome

Why the name works

Hemiko has gone on to establish itself as a leading investor, developer, maintainer and operator of UK town- and city-scale heat networks.
