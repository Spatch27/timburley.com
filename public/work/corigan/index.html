How Corigan came to be.

01 · The Brief

The challenge

Corigan was built as a powerful search engine optimisation platform supporting SEO teams working on larger enterprise clients. Most search-tech tools default to predictable descriptive variations on “Search” and “Rank”, blending into an undifferentiated sea of lookalike software names.

02 · The Story

Inside the naming process

Developed while I was working at the digital agency Organic, the platform needed the confidence to stand on its own feet and build independent brand equity, while preserving a meaningful thread back to its creators. The solution was hiding in plain sight: “Corigan” is an anagram of “Organic”.

03 · Process & Decision

Steering alignment & clearance

By avoiding literal category descriptors, Corigan sidestepped crowded trademark classes and gave the platform immediate personality and authority in client pitches without being tied down to parent-agency dependency.

04 · The Outcome

Why the name works

A crisp, confident platform brand with an insider origin story: distinctive in a noisy MarTech category, legally ownable, and built to grow on its own terms.
